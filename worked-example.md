# Worked Example: Mira and the Lighthouse

## Five lines

- **Character:** Mira, who wants the harbor council to keep the old lighthouse lit through winter.
- **Want:** Present a repair plan before the council votes to close it because repairs are expensive.
- **Cost:** Admit that the lamp’s cracked lens is partly her fault; she dropped it while sneaking inside to look for her missing grandfather’s logbook.
- **Choice:** Tell the truth and offer her own repair work, instead of asking the retired keeper to take the blame.
- **Change:** The council may still close the lighthouse, but the retired keeper can stop being treated as careless, and Mira has a reason to keep researching what the logbook says.

## Drafting prompt

Draft the council scene in 150–250 words. Show Mira choose whether to admit she broke the lens. Use one physical action to make the personal cost visible, and end with a detail that changes how the retired keeper responds.
