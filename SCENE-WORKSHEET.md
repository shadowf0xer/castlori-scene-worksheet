# Five-Line Scene Planner

Use one short phrase or sentence for each line. Focus on one scene and one choice.

1. **Character:** Who makes the choice?
2. **Want:** What do they want to do right now?
3. **Cost:** What might they lose by trying?
4. **Choice:** What action do they take?
5. **Change:** What is different afterward?
